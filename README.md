# ScoreMint

**ScoreMint** is an on-chain prediction and leaderboard platform that lets **anyone** — creators, communities, or even a group of friends — run **fair, transparent prediction challenges**, with or without rewards.

Friends can set up predictions just to **test their football knowledge**, track who’s right most often, and compete on leaderboards — no money required.

For creators and communities, ScoreMint adds optional **creator-funded rewards**, automatic scoring, dispute-proof outcomes, and **NFT badges minted to winners** — all powered by smart contracts.

ScoreMint is **not gambling**.  
There is **no user staking**, **no betting**, and **no financial risk to participants**.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Prediction contests today are either:
- Informal and chaotic (group chats, Twitter replies, spreadsheets)
- Or gambling-based platforms that require staking and financial risk

Friends who just want to test their prediction knowledge have no fair way to:
- Track predictions properly
- Prove who predicted first
- Keep transparent scores over time

Creators face even bigger problems:
- Manual winner selection
- Accusations of bias
- No verifiable proof of fairness
- Growing distrust as audiences scale

---

## 🧠 How ScoreMint Works

### 🔹 Single Prediction Challenge

1. A user creates a prediction event (e.g. *Nigeria vs Ghana*)
2. Optionally locks a reward into a smart contract
3. Shares the prediction link
4. Participants submit predictions **for free**
5. A trusted oracle confirms the final result
6. The smart contract:
   - Identifies correct predictions
   - Uses earliest valid timestamps
   - Updates scores or sends rewards automatically
   - Mints an NFT badge to the winner (if enabled)

No manual work.  
No disputes.  
Fully verifiable.

---

### 🏆 Leaderboards & Ongoing Competitions

ScoreMint also supports long-running competitions.

- Anyone can create a leaderboard
- Competitions can run weekly, monthly, or per tournament
- Participants earn points for correct predictions
- Scores update automatically after each event
- Reward pools (if any) are locked upfront
- Top scorers are settled automatically
- NFT badges are minted for top performers

This turns one-off predictions into **ongoing social challenges**.
