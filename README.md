ScoreMint
Decentralized Prediction and Leaderboard Platform on Base

ScoreMint is a decentralized prediction and leaderboard platform that lets anyone run fair, transparent prediction challenges. Friends can test their football knowledge and compete on leaderboards. Creators and communities can run transparent giveaways with optional creator-funded rewards, automatic scoring, and NFT badges for winners.

ScoreMint is not gambling. There is no user staking, no betting, and no financial risk to participants.

---

Who It's For
ScoreMint is built for:

Friends testing their prediction knowledge
Football creators and influencers
Sports communities and fan groups
Brand engagement campaigns
DAOs and Web3 communities
Our goal is to provide a trustless way to compete on predictions and prove skill through transparent scoring.

---

The Problem
Prediction contests today have limitations:

Informal and Chaotic - Group chats, Twitter replies, and spreadsheets are unreliable
Manual Winner Selection - Creators face accusations of bias
No Verifiable Proof - No way to prove who predicted first or track scores transparently
Gambling-Based Platforms - Require staking and financial risk
ScoreMint solves this by bringing transparency, automatic scoring, and optional rewards on-chain.

---

How It Works
Single Prediction Challenge
Users create prediction events where:

A prediction event is created with optional rewards
Rewards are locked into a smart contract
Participants submit predictions for free
A trusted oracle confirms the final result
The smart contract handles scoring and payouts automatically
NFT badges are minted to winners

Leaderboards and Ongoing Competitions
ScoreMint supports long-running competitions where:

Competitions run weekly, monthly, or per tournament
Participants earn points for correct predictions
Scores update automatically after each event
Reward pools are locked upfront
Top scorers are settled automatically
NFT badges are minted for top performers

Core Concepts

1. On-Chain Timestamps
   All predictions are recorded with immutable timestamps to ensure fairness and prevent disputes about who predicted first.

2. Automatic Scoring
   Smart contracts automatically identify correct predictions, calculate scores, and update leaderboards without manual intervention.

3. Optional Rewards
   Creators can lock rewards into smart contracts upfront. Payouts happen automatically based on results.

4. NFT Badges
   Winners receive NFT badges as on-chain proof of skill and achievement.

5. Transparent Management
   Everything is managed transparently on-chain with public verification of results and no hidden fees.
